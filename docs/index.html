Features

Photo records

Snap photos of clothes you try on and save brand, size, and fit notes together. Easy to look back on later.

Just photograph a clothing tag and AI automatically reads the brand, product name, and size. No more manual entry.

Record how clothes fit — "just right," "a bit tight," "too large" — and use it as a reference for your next purchase.

Found something you love? Tap Search to look up the brand and product online. Go from fitting room to checkout in seconds.
